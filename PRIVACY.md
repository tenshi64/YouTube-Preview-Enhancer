# Privacy Policy for YouTube Preview Enhancer

**Last updated:** November 2026

YouTube Preview Enhancer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or user activity.

## Data Storage
The extension uses the browser's built-in `chrome.storage.sync` API solely to save your local preferences (such as your preferred preview volume level, auto-mute toggle, and closed captions setting) and synchronize them across your signed-in browser profile. This data is managed directly by your browser and is never accessible to the developer or any third parties.

## Third-Party Services
This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking scripts, advertisements, or external servers. All operations are performed locally on YouTube pages (`youtube.com`).

## Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can open an issue on this GitHub repository or reach out via the support link provided in the extension popup.
